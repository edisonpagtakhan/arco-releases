Arco Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Arco is a personal finance tracker. This policy explains what data Arco handles and how, in plain language, and is written to be accurate to how the app actually works.

The short version

  • Arco works fully offline and without an account. If you never sign in, none of your financial data ever leaves your device.
  • Signing in is optional, and it is the only thing that sends data off your device. It exists purely to sync your data across your own devices.
  • Arco shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising or analytics/tracking.

What Arco stores on your device

Your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, and recurring rules are stored locally on your device. Appearance and currency settings are stored locally too, and if you enable App Lock, a PIN verifier (a salted one-way hash; your PIN itself is never stored). Uninstalling the app, or using Reset all data in the app, removes this local data.

What is sent off your device (only if you sign in)

If you create an account and sign in, the following are transmitted, over encrypted connections (HTTPS), to enable cross-device sync:

  • Your email address, to create and authenticate your account.
  • Your financial data (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules), stored in your account so it can appear on your other signed-in devices.

This data is processed by our backend service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting.
  • PowerSync: the sync service that keeps your devices up to date.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Exchange rates

To show amounts in your chosen currency, Arco fetches public exchange-rate data from Frankfurter (European Central Bank reference rates). Only currency codes are requested, so no personal or financial information is sent with these requests.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. App Lock (optional) gates the app with a 6-digit PIN and, where available, biometrics. The PIN is never stored; only a salted PBKDF2 verifier is held in your platform's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

Retaining and deleting your data

  • Local data: use Reset all data in the app, or uninstall.
  • Synced data: signing out clears the copy on that device. Running Reset all data while signed in also clears your data across your other devices.
  • To request deletion of your account and any associated data, contact us at the address below.

Children

Arco is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
